# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 25, 2025**

## Introduction

Save Your Game ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you sign in with Google, we collect:
- Your email address
- Your display name
- Your profile picture (from Google)

### Profile Information
You may provide additional information such as:
- A nickname (required for using the app)
- A custom profile picture (uploaded from your device)

### User-Generated Content
- Game ratings (0 to 5 stars)
- Written reviews and review titles
- Platform selections for each game
- Comments and likes on other users' reviews

### Social Information
- List of users you follow
- List of users who follow you

### Automatically Collected Information
- Device type and operating system version
- App usage data and crash reports
- Advertising identifiers (for personalized ads)

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the app's functionality
- Display your profile and reviews to other users
- Show your activity in your followers' feeds
- Display personalized advertisements
- Improve the app experience
- Send important service-related notifications

## Third-Party Services

### Supabase
We use Supabase as our backend service to store your account data, reviews, and social connections. Their privacy policy is available at: https://supabase.com/privacy

### Google Sign-In
Authentication is handled through Google Sign-In. Google's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Google AdMob
We display advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. Google's advertising privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

### IGDB (Twitch)
Game information (titles, covers, descriptions) is provided by IGDB. Their privacy policy is available at: https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-notice/

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your data may be shared in the following ways:
- **Publicly visible:** Your nickname, profile picture, game ratings, and reviews are visible to other users of the app.
- **Third-party services:** As described above, data is processed by our service providers (Supabase, Google, IGDB).
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose information if required by law.

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please contact us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

Save Your Game is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent for data processing

## Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
